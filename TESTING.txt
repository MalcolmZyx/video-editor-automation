YEAR VIDEO EDITOR - HOW TO TEST (copy/paste each line into PowerShell, one at a time)
=====================================================================================
All commands are run from the project folder:

cd C:\Users\mjzar\Documents\GameJam\video-editor-automation


STEP 0 - Get the latest code and tools (once, and after I tell you I pushed changes)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
git pull
.venv\Scripts\pip install -e ".[dev,themes]"

  ("themes" is the optional picture-grouping add-on. Skip ",themes" if you do not want it.)


STEP 1 - Quick safety check that everything works on your computer (about 3 minutes)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
.venv\Scripts\pytest -q

  Expect:  "... passed" with no failures.


STEP 2 - Try it on FAKE data first (no risk to your files)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
.venv\Scripts\python -m yearvideo demo output\demo

  Opens the editor in your browser (http://127.0.0.1:8765). Several fake months.
  Leave the PowerShell window open while you use it. Press Ctrl+C in it to stop.


STEP 3 - Your real files
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Folder layout (copy your originals in; the tool never edits your originals):

  my-year\
    01-October\
      photos\   (JPGs)
      videos\   (MP4s)
      music\    (the song for that month: mp3 / wav / m4a / flac)
    02-November\
      ...

Name months so they sort in order (01-..., 02-..., or 10-October, 11-November).

Open ONE month:
.venv\Scripts\python -m yearvideo review my-year\01-October

Open the WHOLE YEAR (needed for "Take from other months"):
.venv\Scripts\python -m yearvideo review my-year

After you add/remove files in a month folder by hand:
.venv\Scripts\python -m yearvideo rescan my-year\01-October


STEP 4 - What to try in the editor, in this order
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Dropped list: restore anything that was wrongly dropped as a duplicate.
 2. Videos: click "Trim..." on a clip and check the auto-picked moment.
 3. Group same moments (Storyboard header): puts photos of the SAME moment/event together, placed where
    the first one was taken; everything else stays in time order. Strictness dropdown next to it.
    "Ungroup (by time)" or Ctrl+Z undoes it. First use downloads ~150 MB once.
 4. Media panel (bottom of the page, year folder only): one tab per month, each with its own colour and
    a badge on every picture saying which month it is from; "All months" shows everything. DRAG a picture
    onto the Clips row to drop it exactly there, or double-click it to add it at the playhead, or tick
    several and press "Add". Pictures from another month are MOVED on disk into this month (cut and
    paste) and show "from <month>" with a "send back" button. Faded pictures are already in the timeline.
 5. Combine months (header, "Combine months..."): tick two or more months to edit as ONE month. The
    pictures stay in their folders; the combined month appears in the month list and REPLACES its
    sources in the year video. Coloured month labels and divider lines on the Clips row show which
    month each picture is from, and the big month name sits at the top. "Split back" undoes it.
 5b. Build year video (header): plans/renders whatever changed, then joins everything into year.mp4
    in the year folder (link appears next to the button). One render runs at a time. The month you
    are on is remembered in the address (?m=...), and a second window with an older copy of a
    month is refused (you are offered a reload) instead of overwriting newer edits.
 5c. Reorder the storyboard by dragging a picture's thumbnail onto another card (white bar shows
    whether it lands before or after). Layout: drag the thin bar above the editor to make it taller or
    shorter, drag the vertical edges of the preview / inspector to resize them; double-click a bar to
    reset. Sizes are remembered.
 5d. Sections (Month settings / Storyboard / Dropped / Media): click the arrow or title to collapse; the
    section you are in keeps its buttons pinned at the top. A picture taken from another month goes
    back to its month when you untick keep or delete it.
 5e. Music: "Choose another song" also lists the UNASSIGNED-MUSIC folder of the year; "Move into <month>"
    moves the file into the month's music folder, "-> unassigned" moves it back.
 5f. Safety: if the server stops or a save fails, a red notice appears, edits stay in the page (and a
    draft in the browser), saving retries by itself and offers to restore the draft after a restart.
    Stress check (slow, by hand): python tools/stress_year.py 150 3
 5g. Notifications: the bell in the header keeps every message (errors, finished renders, sent back...)
    until you delete it (one by one or "Delete all"); the red number counts unread ones.
 5h. Shortcuts (press ? in the editor for the list): Space, arrows (1 s, Shift 5 s), Up/Down jump to the
    previous/next cut, Home/End, S split, Delete, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y, Ctrl+S, + - 0 zoom, Ctrl+wheel zoom,
    drag on the ruler to scrub, double-click a video clip to trim, "Snap" makes captions and songs
    snap to the playhead and clip edges. Lengths below 0.2 s are corrected automatically.
 6. Timeline editing: drag clips on the Clips row to reorder, drag block edges to change length,
    press S to split at the playhead, Backspace to delete, Ctrl+Z to undo.
 7. Month settings > Lengths > tick "Manual timing": lengths stay exactly as you set them, the month
    becomes as long as your pictures. "Release all lengths" goes back to automatic.
 7. Music panel: add the song, try beat sync, fades.
 8. Click "Render preview" (fast, small), then "Render final".
 Keyboard: Space = play/pause, Backspace/Delete = remove selected, Ctrl+Z = undo.


STEP 5 - Render from the command line (optional)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
One month:
.venv\Scripts\python -m yearvideo render my-year\01-October

Whole year, joined into one video (my-year\year.mp4):
.venv\Scripts\python -m yearvideo year my-year


NOTE: commands use "python -m yearvideo" instead of "yearvideo" because Windows Application
Control blocks the yearvideo.exe launcher on this PC. Both do the same thing.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Every save keeps a backup next to your plan:  timeline.json.bak
- Red messages in the PowerShell window saying "WinError 10053" are harmless.
- Copy the last ~20 lines of the PowerShell window and send them to me.
